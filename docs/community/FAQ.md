## How do I know I have the real $GOBB and not a copycat?
Anyone can create a token with the name "Gob" and ticker "$GOBB" — this is trivially easy on pump.fun and happens constantly to popular-sounding names. The name and ticker mean nothing on their own. The **contract address** is the only thing that identifies the real token. Always cross-check the CA shown in your wallet or on a DEX against the CA posted on the official website and official X account — not against a link someone DMs you or posts in a comment.
